# 2026-06-29 Internal Iterative Reduction (IIR) — a missing-standard-technique probe

## Rationale

The proven bottleneck is search DEPTH/EBF (depth-12-vs-10 = +185, `project_search_bound_proven`).
The 2026-06-29 `sd_order` data shows ~3/4 of middlegame nodes carry NO TT move (ttlist ~24–27%).
NGN has IID (search.go:1281) but it only fires on **PV** nodes — it does a reduced search to *find*
a move. NGN has NO **IIR** (Internal Iterative Reduction): the modern, search-free technique of
simply reducing depth by one ply at non-PV / cut nodes that reach depth with no hash move. IIR
directly trims the EBF at exactly the data-flagged nodes and is a *missing standard technique*
(NGN's only historical Elo source besides correctness fixes) — higher prior than perturbing the
already-tuned history machinery (all four of those regressed, `2026-06-29-fmc-ordering.md`).

## Instrument

`ebfprobe.py -nodes 400000` depth reached at fixed nodes = the DIRECT EBF measure (deeper at same
nodes = the lever). Deterministic, runs on the Mac with no conflict with the box game-runs. A clean
IIR win = consistent +1–2 depth across positions without endgame regression. (Caveat: the cold-TT
single search has more no-TT-move nodes than a warm-TT game, so the probe may over-fire IIR vs real
games — directional only; the verdict is a self-play SPRT, valid for search-efficiency changes.)

## Verdict — most promising lever of the night; to a games SPRT

cutNode-IIR is the cleanest positive proxy signal of the 2026-06-29 campaign: a uniform +1 middlegame
depth gain on the PROVEN lever (EBF/depth), via a genuinely missing standard technique. The endgame
−2/−4 is a real risk (a phase/material guard is the obvious follow-up if the net is negative), so the
games verdict is required — proxy depth alone is not kept.
