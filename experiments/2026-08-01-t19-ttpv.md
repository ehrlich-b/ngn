## Why this one is not blocked by the T3 gate

Every T3-family LMR-reduction add is gated behind T10 raising FMC (~84% vs elite ~90%), because T3a proved
that **adding** reduction at NGN's ordering quality costs Elo (-7.9). **ttPv reduces LESS** at nodes that were
previously part of a PV, so it is the opposite bet and the gate does not apply. Recorded on 2026-07-26 and
re-affirmed: **T19 is the OPPOSITE bet from T3a, so the T3a precedent does not transfer to it.**

## Implementation — the bit budget, which was the real blocker

`CachedEval` packs to **exactly 64 bits** (move 28 + eval 16 + depth 7 + type 3 + age 10) with nothing spare,
and entry size is **load-bearing** (16 B would quadruple slots and materially cut the mid tree). The audit
offered two ways to free a bit and recommended the smaller blast radius; the patch takes it:

- **age 10 -> 9 bits**, ttPv occupies bit 63. **Entry stays 64 bits.**
- 9 bits = **512 generations against `OldAge = 5`** — still far more headroom than needed, so aging behavior
  is untouched in practice.

Touches `engine/cache.go`, `engine/search.go` **and** `engine/search_stop_test.go`. **Reverting only
`search.go` leaves the tree dirty** — noted because it has bitten before.

**Honest expectation: +2 to +6, and this is a genuinely uncertain one.** It is a standard technique NGN
lacks entirely, which is the transplant thesis's best case; but the campaign has now had a **correctly
transplanted standard technique lose 22.5 Elo** (T18b), so "SF has it and we don't" is not evidence of Elo in
NGN's calibration.

**Leading failure hypothesis if T19 rejects: the ~1-ply bill is simply larger than the accuracy gain.** The
+58.2% middlegame tree growth at fixed depth is the largest of any candidate staged this session, and at
fixed real clock that is paid in depth across the board. **If it rejects, do NOT re-run it with a tweaked
reduction amount** — that is the hand-picked-constant class that has failed repeatedly. The honest reading
would be that de-reduction at PV-marked nodes is not affordable at NGN's current ordering quality, which
would also be **the first real evidence about the de-reduction (level) axis** — the shape axis is already
closed by T6a, and the level axis is explicitly recorded as still untested.

**If T19 keeps:** it is the first member of the de-reduction axis to land, and `cutoffCnt` stays GATED
regardless (it INCREASES reduction — the class T3a falsified).
