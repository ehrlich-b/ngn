## Changes and gates

Every candidate is compared with its immediate parent. `scripts/speedcheck.py`
checks exact nodes, score, best move, and full extracted PV for both diagnostic
fixed search and production iterative search, depth 9, all six positions and
both evaluators: **24/24 identical for each candidate**. Paired timed trials
alternate AB/BA by repeat, position, and evaluator, pinned to CPU 2. Gains below
are geometric means of paired NPS ratios; pooled rates are reported separately.

| Change | Commit | Gate | HCE gain | NNUE gain | Decision |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Direct board relocation; preserve phase, update PST differences | `7291210` | 2 × 3 s/position/side | +1.69% | +6.81% | Keep: NNUE repeats +8.44%, +5.21% |
| Occupied pawn/king iteration; exact passed/shield geometry masks | `5a9a20d` | 2 × 3 s/position/side | +18.73% | -0.76% | Keep: HCE repeats +18.30%, +19.16%; NNUE code path unaffected |
| Paired NNUE feature bases | `135f54d` | 3 × 5 s/position/side | -1.24% | -0.32% | Reverted: no repeatable ≥2% gain |

Pooled NPS at each gate (baseline → candidate): relocation HCE 2.021 → 2.063
MN/s, NNUE 2.049 → 2.192 MN/s; geometry HCE 2.070 → 2.402 MN/s,
NNUE 2.090 → 2.074 MN/s. The discarded feature trial: HCE 2.512 → 2.471
MN/s, NNUE 2.256 → 2.242 MN/s. These short screens select candidates; the
longer final paired run measures total gain.

Relocation: 20,000 randomized comparisons against independent square primitives,
including invalid fields, captures, overlapping masks and castling. Geometry:
exhaustive square-pair masks and 3,000 randomized pawn/king term comparisons
against coordinate enumeration. The reverted feature trial also checked all
256 int8 piece values × 64 squares against the original feature definition. Existing NNUE transition/kernel
and evaluator tests cover promotions, EP, castles and accumulator restoration.
Perft reference suite passed at the relocation and geometry gates.
