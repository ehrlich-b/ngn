# Strength

Official ratings identify the rating list, version, time control, date and game
count. Local estimates calibrate completed games against named, fixed historical
opponent ratings; they are separate from official ratings and depend on those
anchors and the time control. Predeclare the opponents, openings, threads,
sample size or stopping rule, exclusions and confidence calculation before play.
Keep binary/model hashes, per-game results and faults with the protocol.

See the [October 7 calibration](../experiments/2026-10-07-3000-claim-result.md),
the [development campaign](../experiments/2026-10-08-3600-campaign.md), and the
[strength record format](STRENGTH-RECORD.md). A stopped or incomplete run is
not a completed confirmation; relative match results do not establish an
absolute rating.
